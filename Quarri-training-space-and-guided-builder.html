How to use the Quarri training space and guided builder

Learn Quarri on a demo dataset, then build dashboards, apps, alerts and reports on your own data.

Step 1

Sign in

Go to app.quarri.ai and continue with Google or Microsoft, or ask for a sign-in link by email. Passwords still work, with an authenticator app or passkey if you use one.

Step 2 · Learn

Learn to use Quarri in your role, in the Quarri training space

In Claude, type Get started with Quarri and pick the training space. Quarri takes you through it step by step, so you learn to ask questions, build dashboards, set alerts and run reports the way you would in your job.

You practise on your own copy of sample data: an invented business with two years of data on what comes in, what’s made, what’s in stock and what goes out to customers, with public price data beside it. It has the problems a real business has, so there’s something to find. Nothing you do there touches your own data.

After a quick tour, pick a track:

Build track
Set up a dashboard and its alert, an order desk app and a weekly Excel report
Connect track
See how data comes in through a connector and gets organised so Claude can answer questions on it

Your copy is kept for 14 days after you last use it.

Step 3 · Build

Build on your own data with the Quarri guided builder

Before you build. Building is token-intensive, especially dashboards and apps, so it uses up your Claude plan’s usage limits much faster than asking questions does. Keep an eye on your limits, build one thing at a time, and save big builds for when you have the room.

Back in your own workspace, describe what you need in Claude. Quarri’s guided builder plans it with you first, then builds it on your Quarri data. For example:

Plan what to build“What should we build first?”
A dashboard“Build a dashboard of last month’s sales by site”
An app“Make an app where the team logs stock adjustments for approval”
Alerts and scheduled emails“Email me when weekly sales drop more than 10%”
An Excel report“Rebuild this workbook every Monday and send it to the managers”
An Excel export“Export this month’s figures by product to Excel”
A map“Show sales by site on a map”
New data“Bring in daily weather for our locations”
A change“Add last year to the sales dashboard”
Your terms“When we say margin, we mean after freight”

Use Claude Code for dashboards, apps and alerts. Those are built as code, so open Claude Code for them. Everything else works in claude.ai or the Claude desktop app.

Help

When you need us

If Claude reaches something it can’t do, it can send your request straight to the Quarri team. And if you’d like us to take your team through it, email theo@quarri.ai.
