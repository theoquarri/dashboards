What’s new
01 · Quarri training space
Learn to use Quarri in your role
Quarri now has its own training space. Ask Claude to get started with Quarri and it shows you, step by step, how to ask questions, build dashboards, set alerts and run reports in Claude. You practise on a demo sawmill, so nothing touches your real data.
02 · Quarri guided builder
Quarri builds it with you
Describe what you need in Claude. Quarri’s guided builder plans it with you, then builds the dashboard, app, alert or report on your Quarri data.
03 · Quarri maps
Your operation, on a map
Quarri now draws maps in the chat. Shade blocks by any figure, put mills and yards on top, and play the years through.
04 · Sign-in
One-click sign-in
Sign in to Quarri with Google or Microsoft, or open a sign-in link from your inbox.
05 · Security
Multi-factor authentication
Turn on MFA for your own sign-in, or for your whole team, from Security, bottom left in Quarri.
›MFA inside Claude locks Claude after 15 minutes idle until you enter your code. Ask a member of the Quarri team to turn it on.
